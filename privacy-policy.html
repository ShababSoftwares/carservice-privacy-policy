Privacy policy

Used Car Service History Tracker stores the vehicle records you enter on this device.

Data stored on the device

Vehicles, service records, reminders, documents, mileage, expenses, and settings stay in local storage. Photos you attach are copied into the app's private files. This version has no account and no cloud backup. Uninstalling the app removes that copy.

Analytics and crashes

When a Firebase project is connected, Analytics receives feature events and Crashlytics receives crash reports. Those reports do not include registration numbers, VINs, notes, receipts, or document contents. Without google-services.json, Firebase is not initialized.

Advertising

The free plan can show AdMob banners. Premium, confirmed by Google Play, turns ads off. Consent uses Google's User Messaging Platform where required.

Purchases

Google Play processes payment and tells the app which one-time products this account owns. The app does not receive card numbers. A short cache of the last confirmed purchase can apply for a few hours if Play is unreachable.

Notifications and permissions

Reminders are scheduled on the device. The app asks for notification, camera, or photo access only when needed. It does not request location, contacts, or microphone access.

Sharing and deletion

A PDF stays on the device until you share it. It is labeled as a user-maintained history, not a certified record. Deleting a vehicle removes its linked records. Uninstalling the app removes the local database. There is no server copy in this version.
