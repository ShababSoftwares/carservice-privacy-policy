# Privacy Policy for Car Service History

**Last updated: November 17, 2026**

## Introduction

Muhammad Arif Shabab ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Used Car Service History Tracker mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Car Service History does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not operate a backend that stores your vehicles, service records, or purchases. The records you enter stay on your device. Optional premium products are one-time purchases sold and managed by Google Play Billing on Android.

**Your vehicle history stays on the device.** We do not upload registration numbers, VINs, notes, receipts, photos, or documents to a server we operate. A PDF leaves the device only if you share it.

## Information We Collect

### No account

We do not ask you to register, sign in, or give us an email address or username to use the app. We do not create a user account for you.

### Vehicle and service data

Vehicle data is stored only on your device, including:

- Vehicles you add, including names, registration numbers, and VINs you enter
- Service records, reminders, mileage readings, and expenses
- Notes, dates, costs, and settings such as currency and distance unit
- Photos and documents you attach, copied into the app's private files

### Purchases

Premium is an optional one-time in-app product. Lifetime Premium and Multi-Vehicle Premium remove ads and unlock the paid features described in the app. They are available on Android through Google Play Billing. The app talks to Google Play on the device. We do not run our own billing server.

- We do not collect or store your credit card number, bank account, billing address, or Google account password.
- Google processes the payment and keeps the purchase record under the Google account signed in to the Play Store on the device.
- The app asks Google Play Billing, on the device, which of these one-time products that account owns. That check is what unlocks premium and turns ads off.
- The app may keep a short-lived copy of that status on the device so premium can stay available for a few hours if Play cannot be reached. That copy expires. It is not a permanent unlock, and it is not uploaded to a server we operate.
- Restoring a purchase uses the Google account already on the device. You do not create an account in Car Service History to restore it.
- Refunds are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details when you request one.

### Device and usage information

When a Firebase project is connected to this app, Google Firebase may receive information that those services collect by default, such as device model, operating system version, app version, and a Firebase app instance identifier. Feature events we send do not include registration numbers, VINs, workshop notes, receipt images, document contents, or exact mileage values. Events can include that a vehicle was added, a service record was saved, a reminder was completed, mileage was updated, an expense was added, a report was generated, or a purchase result occurred.

If `google-services.json` is not part of the build, Firebase is not initialized and those reports are not sent.

### Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics, when configured, receives crash and error reports so the app can be fixed. Those reports are not given registration numbers, VINs, or document contents.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes:

- **To provide and maintain our Service**: Including showing the vehicle records you store on your device, scheduling reminders on the device, and unlocking premium when Google Play reports an owned product.
- **To provide customer support**: If you contact us, we use the information you send in that message to respond.
- **To improve our Service**: We use feature events and crash reports to understand which parts of the app are used and to diagnose failures.
- **To ensure security**: We use information to detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and other harmful activities. Google Play also applies its own checks to purchases.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local storage only

- Your vehicle and service data is stored on your device. We do not upload it to a server we operate, and the app does not sync it to a cloud account.
- Photos and files you attach are copied into the app's private storage. They are not uploaded by the app.
- A local copy of your premium status, read from Google Play Billing, may also be stored on the device for a few hours. It is not sent to us.
- Uninstalling the app removes the records and the local premium cache stored by the app. A Google Play purchase, if you have one, remains with Google. You can restore it on a device signed in to the same Google account.

### Purchases are not stored by us

- We do not keep a database of buyers, purchase tokens, or payment records.
- Google Play is the source of whether you own a premium product. The app reads that status on the device through Google Play Billing.
- The same Google account on another Android device can restore the purchase through Google Play. Car Service History itself has no login that carries a purchase between devices.

### Notifications and permissions

- If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on the device. No external push service is used. Denying permission does not block the rest of the app.
- The app asks for the camera or photo library only when you attach a picture. It does not request location, contacts, or microphone access.

### Data retention

- Vehicle data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Deleting a vehicle removes the records linked to it.
- Google retains purchase records according to Google's own policies. Refund requests are handled by Google Play.
- Firebase retains analytics and crash data according to Google's policies for those products, when Firebase is configured.

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except in the following circumstances:

### Service providers

We may share information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating the app:

- **Google Play Billing**: When you buy premium, the purchase is handled by Google on the device. Google receives the information needed to process the purchase under the Google account on the device. We do not receive your payment card details.
- **Advertising**: The free plan can show ads through Google AdMob. A premium purchase that Google Play reports as owned turns those ads off. Ads are not shown on data-entry forms or while a report is created. AdMob may collect device and usage data, including the advertising identifier, as described in Google's policies. You can change ad consent choices in the app settings where that option is available. Consent uses Google's User Messaging Platform where required.
- **App analytics and crashes**: When configured, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics help us understand how the app is used and diagnose crashes. Those reports do not include registration numbers, VINs, notes, receipts, or document contents.

### Sharing a report

A PDF is created on the device. It leaves the device only if you share it through the system share sheet. The PDF says the history is user-maintained and not independently verified. We do not receive a copy of that file.

### Legal requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

### Business transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and control

- **View your data**: You can access your vehicle records within the app.
- **Export your data**: You can create a PDF of the history stored on your device. That file stays under your control until you share it. We do not receive a copy.
- **Delete your data**: You can delete a vehicle, service record, reminder, document, mileage reading, or expense in the app. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on that device. There is no account to delete.
- **Purchases**: You can review or restore purchases from the app. Refunds are handled by Google Play, not through an account in this app.

### Communication preferences

- You can turn reminders off in the app settings, or deny notification permission in Android settings.
- You can contact us to request changes to information you have sent us directly, such as a support email.

### Data portability

- You have the right to receive a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used format.
- The PDF report is the copy the app can produce on the device. You can also contact us about data you sent to us directly.
