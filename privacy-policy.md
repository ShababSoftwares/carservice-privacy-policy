# Privacy policy

**Used Car Service History Tracker**

This policy describes the Android app. Vehicle records you enter are private.

## Data stored on the device

The app saves vehicles, service records, reminders, documents, mileage readings, expenses, and settings locally. Photos and files you attach are copied into the app's private storage. This version does not create an account and does not upload those records to a cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes the local copy.

## Analytics

If you add a Firebase project, Firebase Analytics receives product events such as a vehicle being added, a service record being saved, a reminder being completed, mileage being updated, an expense being added, a report being generated, and purchase results. Events do not include registration numbers, VINs, notes, receipt images, document contents, or exact mileage.

## Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics, when configured with this app's `google-services.json`, receives crash reports. Those reports are not given registration numbers, VINs, or document contents. Without that file, Firebase is not initialized.

## Advertising

The free plan can show Google AdMob banners. A premium purchase that Google Play reports as purchased turns ads off. Consent uses Google's User Messaging Platform where required. Ads are not shown on data-entry forms or while a report is created.

## Purchases

Google Play processes payments. The app reads which one-time products the Google account owns. Card details stay with Google. A short cache of the last successful Play answer can keep premium available for a few hours if Play cannot be reached. That cache expires and is not a permanent unlock.

## Notifications

Allowed notifications are scheduled on the device. No external notification service is used. Denying permission does not block the rest of the app.

## Permissions

The app asks for notifications when you opt in, and for the camera or photos only when you attach a picture. It does not request location, contacts, or microphone access.

## Sharing

A PDF is created on the device and leaves the device only if you share it. The PDF says the history is user-maintained and not independently verified.

## Deletion

Delete a vehicle to remove its linked records from the device, or uninstall the app. There is no server copy in this version.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
